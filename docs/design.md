You are a senior product designer, creative frontend engineer, and conversion-focused UX designer.

Build a premium modern website for a professional cleaning company called:

DAWN CLEANERS

The company provides professional cleaning services for:

- Residential homes
- Offices
- Airbnb / BnB properties
- Lodges
- Hotels
- Commercial spaces

The website must NOT look like a generic cleaning-company template.

The visual direction should be inspired by Apple's design philosophy:
- extreme clarity
- strong typography
- generous whitespace
- intentional hierarchy
- restrained use of color
- premium photography
- subtle depth
- smooth transitions
- sophisticated motion
- simple interfaces
- attention to tiny interaction details

IMPORTANT:
Apple is an inspiration for design principles, NOT a request to copy Apple's website, branding, layouts, assets, or exact visual designs.

The final website should feel like a premium modern service brand that could compete with high-end hospitality or facility-management companies.

==================================================
DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
==================================================

Follow these principles:

1. Less but better.

2. Every section must have a clear purpose.

3. Avoid visual clutter.

4. Avoid excessive cards.

5. Avoid excessive rounded containers.

6. Avoid huge text that feels like a startup landing-page template.

7. Avoid unnecessary gradients.

8. Avoid decorative blobs.

9. Avoid random floating shapes.

10. Avoid excessive icons.

11. Avoid generic glassmorphism.

12. Avoid "AI-generated website" aesthetics.

13. Use whitespace aggressively.

14. Use strong typography.

15. Use photography as a major visual storytelling element.

16. Use subtle borders and shadows.

17. Keep the interface elegant and restrained.

The site should feel designed by a strong product designer.

==================================================
ANIMATION SYSTEM
==================================================

Animation is extremely important.

Use TWO animation systems intentionally.

FRAMER MOTION:

Use Framer Motion for:

- navbar interactions
- mobile menu
- buttons
- hover states
- cards
- page transitions
- text reveal
- component-level animations

GSAP + ScrollTrigger:

Use GSAP for:

- hero image parallax
- large image reveals
- scroll storytelling
- horizontal sections
- image scaling
- pinned sections
- sophisticated scroll sequences

Do NOT use GSAP for everything.

Do NOT animate every element.

Motion should communicate hierarchy.

==================================================
ANIMATION RULES
==================================================

Animations should feel:

smooth
slow
intentional
premium

Avoid:

bouncing elements
excessive spring effects
random floating objects
constant movement
large rotations
overly dramatic scaling

Use easing such as:

power2.out
power3.out
power4.out

Use durations approximately:

0.4–0.8 seconds

For major cinematic sections:

1–1.5 seconds

Use staggered text reveals.

Example:

eyebrow
↓
headline
↓
paragraph
↓
CTA

Each should appear slightly after the previous element.

==================================================
FINAL EXPERIENCE
==================================================

When a visitor lands on Dawn Cleaners, the immediate impression should be:

"This is a professional company."

After 5 seconds:

"I understand what they do."

After 15 seconds:

"I trust them."

After scrolling:

"I understand their services and who they serve."

At the end:

"I should request a quote."

Design the entire experience around that journey.

Before writing large amounts of code, establish the visual system and component architecture.

Then implement the homepage carefully, section by section.

Prioritize quality over quantity.
